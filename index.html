What the scenario suggests

01 | Model economics

Routing may matter more than raw usage

Open-Compact costs $0.07 per task versus $1.75 for Nova-Large in this simulation. Their simulated success rates are close: 81.7% and 79.8%.

Hypothesis only: task mix differs across models. A matched quality-controlled routing pilot is needed before changing production defaults.

02 | Where to investigate

Engineering has the highest modeled spend

Engineering accounts for $1,772.61 in simulated API spend and has the lowest departmental ROI proxy in this scenario: 414.1%.

That makes token-heavy engineering workflows a review priority, not proof that the team is overspending.

03 | Business interpretation

Capacity value is not cash savings

The 565.5% aggregate result assumes estimated time saved can be valued at a fully loaded labor rate. It does not show that the company avoided cost or earned more revenue.

Validate redeployment, output quality, rework, and realized financial outcomes with Finance.

Data integrity note: Every row, model name, rate, outcome, and time-saving estimate in this demo is synthetic. The scenario demonstrates a method; it is not evidence about real companies or current vendor pricing.

Decision I would recommend

Run a controlled pilot on a narrow set of high-volume workflows. Compare matched AI-assisted and non-AI tasks, track success and rework, calculate billed token cost, and record whether reclaimed time becomes measurable business output. Scale only where quality and realized value hold up.

Implementation: SQLite schema and analysis queries, deterministic PowerShell data generation and QA, and a dependency-free HTML dashboard.

Portfolio demonstration. Replace simulated inputs with privacy-reviewed company telemetry and finance-approved assumptions before making investment decisions.
